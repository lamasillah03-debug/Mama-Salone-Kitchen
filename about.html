The Story Behind
Mama Salone Kitchen

Mama Salone Kitchen was born from a simple idea – to bring the authentic taste of Sierra Leone to our community. Our restaurant is more than just a place to eat; it's a place where family, friends, and good food come together.

Inspired by traditional recipes and the rich culture of our homeland, we prepare every dish with fresh ingredients, love, and care. We want every customer to feel at home and enjoy the true flavours of Salone.
